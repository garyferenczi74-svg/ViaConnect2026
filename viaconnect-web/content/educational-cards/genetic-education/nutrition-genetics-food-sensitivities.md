# Food sensitivities: genetics and gut context

## This is not an allergy test and it is not a celiac diagnosis.

Lactase persistence research, HLA IDs listed on the extract, GAD1 glutamate-to-GABA research, and gut scores may appear together. Genetics is one piece beside symptoms and medical history. This card lists extract HLA rs IDs only. It does not interpret HLA for a food plan. It does not prescribe food. IgE-mediated food allergy is a medical diagnosis.

This card covers the confirmed extract list (19 unique rs IDs). Your genetics report header may list 20 variants. This extract includes only the confirmed variants named below.

Confirmed variants: GAD1 rs3749034, rs12185692, rs3791878, rs3828275; MCM6 rs4988235; APOA2 rs5082; TAGAP rs1738074; IL-18RAP rs917997; RGS1 rs2816316; IL-12A-AS1 rs17810546; CCR3 rs6441961; ATXN2 rs653178; IL-21 rs13119723, rs6822844; HLA rs4639334, rs4713586; HLA-DQB1 rs7775228; HLA-DRA rs2395182; HLA rs2187668.

Histamine / DAO genes live on Additional Nutrition Genetics in this extract, not here.

## Lactose: MCM6 / LCT persistence vs a breath-test diagnosis

MCM6 rs4988235 (often called C/T-13910) is a European lactase-persistence variant (Enattah et al., Nature Genetics, 2002). It is not a lactose intolerance diagnosis. A persistence marker is not the same as a hydrogen breath-test diagnosis. NIDDK describes breath testing and a clinician-guided food trial as clinical tools. Other ancestries have other persistence variants that are not on this confirmed list.

## HLA: extract rs IDs only

The confirmed HLA tags on this card are rs4639334, rs4713586, rs7775228, rs2395182, and rs2187668. They are listed as present on the extract. This card does not interpret HLA or prescribe food.

If a celiac workup is already planned, follow the clinician. This page does not prescribe food.

Related immune-region tags on this confirmed list (TAGAP, IL-18RAP, RGS1, IL-12A-AS1, CCR3, ATXN2, IL-21) stay as listed research SNPs.

## GAD1 activity

GAD1 variants appear in glutamate-to-GABA conversion research. Anecdotes about MSG or grain sensitivity are unproven. This is not an anxiety diagnosis.

## Dairy-fat weight stories (APOA2)

APOA2 rs5082 also appears on hunger and saturated-fat cards. Research discusses saturated fat and body-mass scores in some cohorts. That is an association, not a dairy ban.

## Gut scores and stool markers as clinician context

Intestinal permeability scores, stool markers, or zonulin, if already on file, are clinician context. They are not leaky gut treatment. Food lists such as broth, glutamine, or low-FODMAP are not therapy ordered from this card.

## What to do if you already have an IgE allergy or a planned celiac workup

If you have hives, swelling, or breathing symptoms after a food, use your emergency plan and a clinician, not this report. Known IgE allergy: do not contradict medical avoidance. Children: pediatric GI or allergy owns workups. Do not stack multiple avoid lists.

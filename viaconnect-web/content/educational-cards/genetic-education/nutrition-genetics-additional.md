# Extra nutrition genes: histamine pathways and bitter taste

## Leftover SNPs are still education, not a supplement protocol.

This partner section holds nutrition-related variants that do not yet have their own category. In the confirmed extract that means AOC1 (DAO), HNMT, HDC (histamine pathway), and TAS2R38 (bitter taste). That is not a methylation stack. MTHFR, VDR, and BCO1 are not on this confirmed list. Do not write a methylation card from this slug.

This card covers the confirmed extract list (10 unique rs IDs), which matches the partner header.

Confirmed variants: AOC1 rs10156191, rs2052129, rs1049742, rs1049793, rs2071514; HNMT rs1050891, rs11558538; HDC rs2073440; TAS2R38 rs713598, rs1726866.

## Why additional exists

The partner page says these SNPs do not yet have their own category. Treat the section as info-level. This page does not recommend a brand or dose.

## DAO / HNMT / HDC as histamine-pathway research

AOC1 encodes diamine oxidase (DAO). AOC1 variants are discussed as DAO activity research, not as a disease (PMID 34651098). HNMT and HDC sit in histamine methylation and synthesis research. Reviews describe histamine-intolerance evidence as mixed. Maintz and colleagues reported AOC1 variants associated with DAO activity but not with a histamine-intolerance phenotype by themselves.

Partner tooltip examples that quote an odds ratio for migraine are research-style marketing. Do not quote odds ratios as the user's risk. This page does not diagnose histamine intolerance. AOC1 is not a migraine diagnosis.

Food-first conversation is allowed. A strict low-histamine diet order is not. Do not start DAO capsules from this card. Do not stack avoidance lists.

## TAS2R38 and vegetable bitterness

TAS2R38 rs713598 and rs1726866 are among the variants that sort bitter taste of PROP and PTC in research. Some people find cruciferous vegetables more bitter. That is preference, not a nutrient deficiency. Preparation (roasting, pairing with acid or fat) is a conversation, not a reason to skip plants.

# Extra nutrition genes: histamine pathways and bitter taste

## These extra variants are still education, not a supplement protocol.

This section groups nutrition-related variants that do not yet have their own category. On this extract that means AOC1 (DAO), HNMT, HDC (histamine pathway), and TAS2R38 (bitter taste). That is not a methylation panel. MTHFR, VDR, and BCO1 are not on this confirmed list.

This card covers the confirmed extract list (10 unique rs IDs), which matches the report header.

Confirmed variants: AOC1 rs10156191, rs2052129, rs1049742, rs1049793, rs2071514; HNMT rs1050891, rs11558538; HDC rs2073440; TAS2R38 rs713598, rs1726866.

## Why this group exists

These variants do not yet have their own category. This page is information only. It does not recommend a brand or dose.

## DAO / HNMT / HDC as histamine-pathway research

AOC1 encodes diamine oxidase (DAO). AOC1 variants are discussed as DAO activity research, not as a disease (PMID 34651098). HNMT and HDC sit in histamine methylation and synthesis research. Reviews describe histamine-intolerance evidence as mixed. Maintz and colleagues reported AOC1 variants associated with DAO activity but not with a histamine-intolerance phenotype by themselves.

An odds ratio in research is not your personal migraine risk. This page does not diagnose histamine intolerance. AOC1 is not a migraine diagnosis.

You can talk about food first. A strict low-histamine diet order is not what this card does. Do not start DAO capsules from this card. Do not stack avoidance lists.

## TAS2R38 and vegetable bitterness

TAS2R38 rs713598 and rs1726866 are among the variants that sort bitter taste of PROP and PTC in research. Some people find cruciferous vegetables more bitter. That is preference, not a nutrient deficiency. Preparation (roasting, pairing with acid or fat) is a conversation, not a reason to skip plants.

## Folate, B12, and vitamin D are not on this list

If folate, B12, or vitamin D comes up, those nutrients have their own education cards. The genes often discussed with those nutrients are not on this confirmed additional list.

## Key takeaways

- A research odds ratio is not your personal risk.
- This page does not recommend a brand or dose.
- NIH ODS remains a public reference for micronutrient facts.
- This card's 10 confirmed rs IDs match the header: AOC1, HNMT, HDC, TAS2R38 only.
- No elimination diet from taste or histamine SNPs.

## What to do next

If headaches, flushing, or food reactions are a problem, see a clinician. Do not start DAO capsules or a strict avoidance list from this card. Bitter-taste variants can be a conversation about trying vegetables in different preparations, not about skipping plants.

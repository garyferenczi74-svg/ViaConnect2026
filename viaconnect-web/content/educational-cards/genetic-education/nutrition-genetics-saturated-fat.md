# Saturated fat, lipids, and APOE context

## Saturated fat is a food-pattern topic. Genes do not replace a lipid clinic.

Saturated fat appears in higher amounts in some meats, dairy fats, coconut, and many baked or fried packaged foods. Dietary Guidelines discuss replacing some saturated fat with unsaturated fat as a pattern. APOA2 is a classic nutrigenetic saturated-fat research example. APOE coding variants are sometimes shown for lipid education. They are not a dementia diagnosis on this page.

This card covers the confirmed extract list (16 unique rs IDs). Your genetics report header may list 19 variants. This extract includes only the confirmed variants named below.

Confirmed variants: ABCA1 rs2230806; ACE rs4343; AGT rs699; APOA1 rs670; APOA2 rs5082; ApoB rs693; ApoC3 rs5128; APOE rs429358; APOE rs7412; APOE rs405509; IL6 / IL6-AS1 rs1800795; LIPC rs1800588; PPARG rs1801282; PPARG2 rs10865710; TCF7L2 rs7903146; TNF rs1800629.

## Saturated fat in foods (Dietary Guidelines tone)

Dietary Guidelines discuss limiting foods higher in saturated fat as part of a population pattern, and replacing some saturated fat with unsaturated fat. That is pattern language. Extreme saturated-fat restriction is not implied. Pediatric and eating-disorder clients stay on pattern language only.

## APOA2 and saturated fat in research

APOA2 rs5082 is a well-known research example. Corella, Ordovas, and colleagues reported a gene-diet interaction with saturated fat intake and body-mass scores in several cohorts. It is one association, not a rule for every person. NIH does not genotype-guide saturated fat.

## APOE on a nutrition page: lipids only

APOE epsilon status is a haplotype of rs429358 and rs7412. In general education, the common alleles are called e2, e3, and e4. e3 is the most common. MedlinePlus describes e2, e3, and e4 as versions of a protein that helps carry cholesterol. This page does not compute or announce a personal haplotype. If your genetics report later shows an APOE band, treat that display as report context, not as a diagnosis on this page.

rs405509 is a promoter SNP and is not the epsilon pair. Do not treat it as e4/e4.

This nutrition page stays in lipids. Cognitive-disease claims, including Alzheimer causation language, are off limits here.

## Inflammation-adjacent SNPs stay in metabolic context

IL6 rs1800795 and TNF rs1800629 appear on this confirmed list. They stay in metabolic and lipid research context. They do not diagnose an inflammatory disease.

TCF7L2 rs7903146 is a widely studied type 2 diabetes research locus. On this extract it is confirmed on saturated fat, not on the insulin-resistance confirmed list. Carrying a studied variant is not a diabetes diagnosis.

## Labs guide medical decisions; genes do not start a statin

Lipid and ApoB results, if on file, guide medical decisions. Genes do not start or stop a statin. This page does not set an HDL target. If HDL ranges differ, use the range on your lab report or omit the number.

## Key takeaways

- Extreme saturated-fat restriction is not implied.
- This page does not announce a personal APOE haplotype.
- This page does not set an HDL target from conflicting report ranges.
- This card covers 16 confirmed rs IDs. Extra header slots are not on this extract.
- APOE education here is lipids only.

## What to do next

If the meal plan already shifts fat quality, that is the educational idea. Bring lipid and ApoB results to a clinician. This card is not smoking-cessation or alcohol treatment.

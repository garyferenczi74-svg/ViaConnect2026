# Proposed privacy policy additions (Prompt 212)

**Status:** Proposed for Gary / legal review. Do not apply silently.

## Add under Health and fitness data (or new Connected wearables section)

### WHOOP

When you connect WHOOP, ViaCura receives recovery, sleep, workout, and related biometric readings through WHOOP's cloud API using OAuth you authorize. Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted server-side. You can disconnect WHOOP at any time and optionally delete stored WHOOP wearable rows.

### Apple Health and Google Health Connect

When you grant ViaCura permission to read Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), we may receive heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, energy, and body composition samples that other apps (including Hume Health) have written to the phone health store. We use this data only to personalize your Bio Optimization Score and related wellness experience. We do not use HealthKit or Health Connect data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

### Retention and deletion

Wearable data is retained while your account is active. Disconnecting a source stops new collection. Choosing "disconnect and delete" removes raw and normalized wearable rows for that provider from our systems, with an audit log of the deletion request.
